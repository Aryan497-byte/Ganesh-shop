Our Story

Ganesh Shop was started in 2009 by Ramesh Shewale with a dream to serve the people of Shivaji Nagar with quality groceries at affordable prices. What started as a small corner shop has now grown into one of the most trusted Kirana stores in the locality.

We believe that every family deserves the best quality at fair prices — and we live by this every single day. Our relationships with our customers are what drive us forward.
